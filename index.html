About Us

TrustReg Pharma Co., Ltd. is a professional pharmaceutical regulatory and business support company based in Myanmar, providing comprehensive, end-to-end solutions for pharmaceutical manufacturers, importers, and healthcare businesses. We specialize in supporting both international and local partners with regulatory affairs, product registration, documentation compliance, and market entry strategy, ensuring smooth, transparent, and fully compliant operations in Myanmar, across Asia, and worldwide markets. With strong technical expertise and practical experience in pharmaceutical regulatory systems, our team is committed to delivering services that are precise, reliable, and aligned with international regulatory standards. Our mission is to simplify complex regulatory frameworks and support pharmaceutical companies in successfully bringing safe, effective, and high-quality healthcare products to global markets.

Our MISSION

To provide high-quality, reliable, and fully compliant pharmaceutical regulatory services that support global healthcare access and sustainable business growth.

Our VISSION

To become a trusted global partner in pharmaceutical regulatory affairs and healthcare business solutions, recognized across Asia and worldwide for excellence, integrity, and regulatory expertise.

OUR SERVICES

We offer a complete range of pharmaceutical regulatory and business support services, including:

Regulatory Affairs Services

  • Product registration (FDA submission support) for pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, health supplements, and medical devices
  • Technical dossier preparation and scientific document review
  • Labeling, packaging, and artwork compliance in accordance with regulatory requirements
  • Product variation, renewal, and lifecycle management applications

Import & Distribution Support

  • Import documentation and regulatory clearance assistance
  • Local compliance advisory and regulatory interpretation
  • Market entry strategy and execution support in Myanmar and regional markets

Business Development Support

  • Strategic partner identification and global networking support
  • Market research, feasibility analysis, and regulatory intelligence
  • Product launch planning and commercialization support

Documentation & Compliance Services

  • Preparation of complete technical and regulatory documentation packages
  • Regulatory guideline consultation and interpretation
  • Quality compliance advisory and submission readiness support

WHY CHOOSE US

  • In-depth expertise in Myanmar FDA regulatory framework
  • Strong understanding of Asian and international regulatory requirements
  • Fast, accurate, and compliant documentation services
  • Strong communication and client-focused coordination
  • Pharmaceutical industry experience with regulatory insight
  • Commitment to integrity, quality, and global compliance standards

We serve as a strategic bridge between international pharmaceutical companies and regulatory authorities in Myanmar, Asia, and worldwide markets, ensuring smooth and compliant global market access.
